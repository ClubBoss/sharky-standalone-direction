# Opponent types · pilot (VI-37)

**Goal:** practise changing one decision when the opponent's stated tendency changes, while the cards, position, stacks, action history and price stay the same.

| Type | Typical tendency | What changes in the decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tight-passive (nit) | Enters relatively few hands; often checks or calls, and rarely raises without strong value. | Check more marginal hands when weaker pairs seldom pay; give rare raises more credit, subject to the call price. |
| Tight-aggressive (TAG) | Plays relatively few starting hands and often bets or raises with selected hands; may release marginal pairs against larger bets. | Choose a smaller value size when it keeps weaker pairs in; check when only stronger hands tend to continue. |
| Loose-aggressive (LAG) | Plays more hands and frequently bets or raises, including some missed draws. | A raising range may contain more hands you beat; reconsider a fold if the modeled equity covers the call price. |
| Loose-passive (calling station) | Plays more hands and often calls with weaker pairs; generally raises less often. | Value bet more readily when weaker hands pay, possibly for a larger size; reassess when this player makes an unusual raise. |
| Very loose-aggressive (maniac) | Enters many hands and applies unusually frequent aggression, including with weak hands. | Consider continuing against a wider modeled raising range when the price warrants it; do not call every raise. |

**Honesty rule:** these are tendencies, not fixed identities, certain reads, or universal frequencies. A passive player can bluff; an aggressive player can have a strong hand. The pilot states a finite range or response model before each choice; its calculations apply only to that study model. The style name alone never determines the answer.

**Pilot:** one lesson, six paired river spots, twelve drills: three value pairs (`value_vs_frequent_caller`) and three rare-raise pairs (`respect_rare_raise`). Each pair changes the best offered action. Both principles are proposed for the later catalog integration; no new table visuals, HUD, routes or app behavior are part of B146b.
