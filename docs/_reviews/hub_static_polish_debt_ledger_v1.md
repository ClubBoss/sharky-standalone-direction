## Recommended next owner layer: C — Sharky identity

**Why now:** Sharky placeholder identity is the only explicitly P1 debt before
Human QA/public consideration. The completed hub/table/Profile passes improved
structure and proof framing; without a resolved companion identity, further
static-card polish has lower incremental EV and risks decorating a system whose
most recognisable product layer is still incomplete.

**Expected EV:** high. A coherent, production-ready Sharky identity can lift
recognition and emotional continuity across existing approved touchpoints
without inventing a new progression system.

**Risk:** medium-high if implementation starts before final art compatibility,
fallback, scale, and reduced-motion requirements are specified. The next task
should therefore begin with an identity execution audit/spec, not broad asset
replacement.

**Evidence:** controlled compact-phone comparison screenshots are required for
any source/asset implementation. They are not required for the initial
audit/spec.

**First task type:** audit/spec first; source implementation only after the
identity execution contract, asset source, fallback, and consumer list are
accepted.
