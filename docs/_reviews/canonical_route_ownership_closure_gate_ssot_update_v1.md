## Exact Rules Added

No world score, closure, readiness, Human QA readiness, or progression
admission claim may improve until this canonical learner chain is proven:

`App launch -> Home/Learn entry -> required lesson/task -> teaching -> guided practice -> independent assessment -> feedback -> repair -> targeted recheck -> completion -> next task/lesson/world`

Each world must identify and reconcile canonical owners for route entry,
content source, progression, completion, telemetry, repair, targeted recheck,
payoff, and next-step routing.

Optional, debug-support, legacy-blocked, dormant, archived, and parallel flows
may inform risk analysis, but they do not contribute to canonical scores,
closure, Human QA readiness, W1-W12 progression admission, or release readiness
unless they are proved to be required by the normal learner route.

Source existence, manifest inclusion, assets, non-primary reachability,
debug/support reachability, tests against a noncanonical owner, completion
persistence outside canonical progress, and telemetry from noncanonical owners
are insufficient by themselves.

## Required Defect-Class Scan

Future closure packets must check:

1. parallel route owners;
2. content exists but is not progression-required;
3. teaching in one flow and assessment in another;
4. completion written outside canonical progress;
5. telemetry emitted by a noncanonical owner;
6. repair receipts without reachable canonical recheck;
7. score claims based on optional/debug/legacy surfaces;
8. canonical UI using a different content owner than the audited source;
9. duplicate or competing progression owners;
10. learner-reachable but nonrequired side paths mistaken for curriculum.

## W1-W6 Closure Sequence

W1-W6 closure is frozen to this order:

1. canonical route-ownership map for W1-W6;
2. canonical-only deep-learning re-audit;
3. bounded repair of confirmed canonical gaps;
4. closure or proof-of-nonissue for learner-facing P2 findings;
5. reconcile title, job, and identity drift affecting learning;
6. evidence-backed re-score for each world;
7. fixed-build novice Human QA;
8. final bounded repair if QA finds material gaps;
9. hard close only when every W1-W6 world is individually at least 9/10.

No average may compensate for an under-9/10 world. P3 tooling is optional unless
it affects learner quality, canonical proof, or audit repeatability.

## W7-W12 Admission Sequence

W7-W12 must run the canonical route-ownership gate before any deep content
audit or implementation expansion:

1. prove canonical learner entry;
2. prove required reachability;
3. prove progression owner;
4. prove completion owner;
5. prove telemetry owner;
6. prove repair and targeted-recheck owner;
7. prove payoff and next-world routing;
8. exclude optional/debug/legacy evidence from closure and score claims;
9. only then assess content depth.

This must happen earlier for W7-W12 than it did for W1-W6 because the W1
showdown correction exposed the cost of auditing a noncanonical pipeline as if
it were the active curriculum.

## Why This Avoids Future False Closure

The rule forces future reviews to prove the whole learner chain and its owners
before assigning score movement or closure credit. That blocks three common
false positives: content authored in a side path, tests passing against a
noncanonical owner, and progress/telemetry being written outside the active
learner route.
