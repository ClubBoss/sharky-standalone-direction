# Moderator Script

Read only the text below. Do not teach poker or reveal an answer before a choice is made.

1. “Please use the app as you normally would. Say aloud what you are looking for and what you expect to happen.”
2. “Start wherever you think makes sense.” Record the first meaningful action and time from hand-off.
3. During the table and decision, ask only: “What are you looking at now?” If stuck, use the intervention ladder in the scorecard.
4. After feedback: “What do you think the app is telling you?”
5. At Review: “What would you do next?” Do not name the repair CTA.
6. After the retry and return, ask: “What changed?”
7. Ask the four payoff questions verbatim from the observer checklist.

End the task when the learner reaches an understandable continuation state or abandons. Do not ask satisfaction questions until all behavioral observations are complete.

Do not instruct the learner to choose an incorrect answer during the primary
journey. Record a naturally exposed wrong decision and repair loop when it
occurs; do not manufacture one for this formative protocol.
