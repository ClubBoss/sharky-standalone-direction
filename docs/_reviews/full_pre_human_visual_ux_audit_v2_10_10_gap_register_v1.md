## 2. Plain-Language Summary

Volume I is close to Human-QA-ready but not there yet. The core shell (Home, Learn, Welcome, feedback tone, terminal closure, claim-safety) is genuinely strong: the known P1 fixes are confirmed landed in the pixels (no "36-world path" claim, "Open first lesson" CTA, "Use the table, then retry." wrong-feedback copy, no "Blinds"/"80% accuracy" gate anxiety in Session Summary, correct "1 error" singular), the W7-W12 visual chrome is fully consistent with the rest of the app (no legacy/blue/archive rendering), and the terminal/no-W13 state reads as an intentional, earned close rather than a dead end.

But one systemic, high-confidence, machine-fixable defect would very likely corrupt Human QA signal: every single W7-W12 "copy detail" feedback screen (8 of 8 captured) displays a raw internal snake_case identifier string (e.g. `w7_combo_density_visible_card_removal`, `w9_price_intuition_call_price`, `volume_i_terminal_review`) directly in the user-visible feedback panel. A paid tester will notice this immediately, and it will contaminate their judgment of the actual pedagogical content quality with an "this looks buggy/unfinished" reaction that has nothing to do with the curriculum itself. A second, related finding — every copy-detail screen's primary CTA button renders with no visible label text — is potentially just as serious but needs live-render verification before being treated as confirmed, since this project's history includes at least two prior false-positive capture-tooling incidents.

Beyond that headline finding, there is a cluster of smaller, bounded, high-EV issues: the Practice category grid exposes an unlocked-looking "Blinds" tile (a direct hit on the audit's own special check #8), a Review-tab screen shows the internal-sounding label "Legal actions" instead of natural copy, the Welcome handoff still carries a large (~55-60%) empty void below its CTA despite a prior fix claiming this was resolved, the Home/Day-2-return header shows "Today 0/3" text next to an almost-fully-filled progress bar (a numeric/visual mismatch), and CTA button styling is inconsistent (solid-blue vs. ghost/outline) for what should be the same action across screens.

What is merely polish, not blocking: the Review tab's large dead space below its single card, Profile's duplicated "Proof profile" heading and generic "Small wins Sharky can prove" tagline, Placement's dead space before its footer CTA, and minor jargon-adjacent phrasing ("no-bet-yet clue," "Meet the table"). These are real 10/10-opportunity gaps but do not threaten Human QA signal integrity the way the internal-id leakage and the "Blinds" grid exposure do.
