## 12. Definition of Ready

1. Three to five eligible novice testers are identified and scheduled.
2. One observer/evidence owner is assigned.
3. A fixed commit/build hash and supported device configuration are recorded.
4. The accepted W1 Human QA protocol and Premium Beta checklist are unchanged
   or any conflict is explicitly adjudicated before the first session.
5. No P0/P1 regression is present in the chosen build.
6. Participants can complete the session without product-author coaching.

## 13. Definition of Done

1. Every scheduled session has the required non-sensitive evidence fields.
2. Comprehension, table-signal recognition, repair explanation, recall, and
   continuation intent are recorded without synthetic substitution.
3. Findings are classified as pass, needs repair, or blocked with P0-P3
   severity and exact owner references.
4. Results are written to
   `docs/_reviews/w1_human_qa_execution_results_v1.md`.
5. No score, launch, or learning-effect claim moves beyond the evidence.
6. Any implementation follow-up is separately admitted from a reproduced
   defect and includes a regression lock.

## 14. Stop Conditions

  content.
