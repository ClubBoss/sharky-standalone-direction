## 1. Verdict

`DUAL_REGISTER_ICONIC_COMPANION_SELECTED`

The active Sharky character strategy is corrected from a serious-premium,
globally restrained mentor trajectory to a dual-register iconic companion:

- calm in learning;
- expressive in progress and milestones;
- playful in marketing;
- recognisable everywhere.

Sharky is not a cold premium mentor and not a preschool pet. Sharky remains
unmistakably shark, warm, intelligent, compact, animation-friendly, and capable
of both calm coaching and memorable celebration.

## 2. Why this correction exists

The previous serious-premium exploration correctly identified real defects in
the legacy Sharky system: weak shark recognisability, generic silhouette,
preschool proportions, weak small-size brand mark, anxious repair expression,
collapsed Confirm/Improve distinction, and low growth expressiveness.

It over-corrected toward a permanently serious, restrained mentor. That creates
new strategic risk: reduced emotional attachment, reduced animation range,
weaker marketing/story/short-form potential, weaker merch and cosmetic
potential, and weaker long-term character IP.

## 4. Register Architecture

### Product / Learning Register

Used in lessons, decision screens, repair feedback, summaries, review, and
practice.

Tone: calm, focused, supportive, low-energy, emotionally safe, and free of
distracting spectacle.

Motion: small settle, subtle eye movement, slight head inclination, controlled
acknowledgement, and restrained repair response.

### Progress / Milestone Register

Used in world completion, session summary, earned proof, milestones, and return
moments.

Tone: visibly happier, more energetic, memorable, and still premium.

Allowed future motion includes short swim-in, small leap, orbit around a
result, reveal from behind a card or panel, joyful fin gesture, or short
celebratory beat.

### Marketing / Story Register

Used in social shorts, animated ads, collaborations, memes, mini-stories,
promotional videos, and merch campaigns.

Tone: bold, playful, witty, cinematic, and culturally flexible.

Motion and props may be broader here if the base identity remains recognisable.

## 5. Base Identity And Accessory Policy

The base Sharky identity must remain recognisable without accessories. It must
come from silhouette, face, dorsal signature, body proportions, movement
grammar, and colour system.

Base identity must not depend on a hoodie, glasses, hat, medal, poker chips,
playing cards, costume, badge, or any other permanent accessory.

Optional earned cosmetics may be considered later as overlays only: small pin,
scarf, temporary celebratory crown, seasonal cosmetic, themed accessory, or
unlocked reaction/pose. They must not create pay-to-win meaning, rarity
theatre, asset-family explosion, emotional-readability interference, or a
replacement for real learning payoff.

Marketing may use broader costumes and props, but those uses do not redefine
canonical base identity.

## 6. Body, Emotion, And Motion Direction

New body target:

- compact;
- clearly shark;
- soft central mass without preschool proportions;
- animation-friendly;
- expressive pectoral fins;
- elastic but controlled tail geometry;
- readable at runtime sizes;
- suitable for full-body marketing animation;
- no human legs.

The product state set still requires Neutral, Coach, Supportive Repair,
Confirm, Improve, and Milestone. The global character range is broader than
that in marketing and story contexts, including surprise, suspicion, playful
smugness, comic frustration, curiosity, excitement, determination, relief, and
exaggerated celebration. These broader emotions must not leak into sensitive
repair moments.

The prior global motion restriction is replaced by register-specific rules:
restrained semantic micro-motion in learning surfaces, short expressive
celebration in progress surfaces, and full character-animation range in
marketing. Reduced-motion parity remains mandatory inside the application.
