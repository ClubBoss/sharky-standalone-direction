## 6. B3 product objective

Transform B1/B2's abstract character-ready player volumes into an original,
coherent premium player-embodiment system.

Target transformation:

`premium poker room containing abstract seat blobs`

->

`premium poker learning scene visibly inhabited by players, with the learner spatially seated among them`

The first-glance perception should move materially closer to:

`I am sitting at a poker table with other players.`

Do not clone PokerSkill characters.

## 7. B3 ownership

B3 owns:

- final intended player embodiment language;
- head / torso / shoulder silhouette;
- player scale by depth;
- cropping / occlusion by rail;
- near / mid / far embodiment;
- original visual character identity;
- player differentiation sufficient to make the scene feel inhabited;
- active / inactive / folded visual state where current semantics support it;
- hero foreground embodiment;
- compatibility with B4 HUD anchors;
- readability under the B2 shared lighting system.

B3 may create original repository-compatible visual assets if that is the
highest-EV approach.

Do not constrain the solution to `CustomPainter` if asset-backed characters are
materially better.

Constraints:

- no copyrighted character assets;
- no PokerSkill copies;
- no unrelated dependencies without causal need.

## 10. B3 iteration strategy

Default to two strong internal iterations.

### Iteration 1 - player family + depth embodiment

Goal: replace abstract volumes with an original coherent player embodiment
system that survives far/mid/near depth.

### Iteration 2 - hero + state integration + cohesion

Goal: make the learner belong to the same visual world and ensure characters
behave correctly through theory, decision, correct, wrong, repair, and recheck
states.

Allow iteration 3 only for one remaining major B3 class.

Do not spend iteration budget on facial micro-detail.

## 11. B3 acceptance bar

B3 is insufficient if:

- the scene still reads as empty chairs or blobs;
- characters look like generic UI avatars pasted around a table;
- far/mid/near players do not share one depth logic;
- the hero remains a detached badge;
- player art overwhelms cards, actions, or teaching.

A successful B3 should immediately create:

`people are sitting around this table`

while preserving:

`this is still a clear poker trainer.`
