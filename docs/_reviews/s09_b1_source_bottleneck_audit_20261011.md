- `first_table_guide_one_clear_choice` (`hero_btn_preflop_setup`: identify position/setup) vs `actions_fold_drill` (fold as an action under a weak continuing context): **UNPROVEN**. Distinct setup-vs-action signals; neither task's ID or course ordering proves independent transfer from one to the other. No W1 content/engine edits admitted by this audit.
