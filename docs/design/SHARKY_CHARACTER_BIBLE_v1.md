# Sharky Character Bible v1

Status: ACTIVE visual identity SSOT. Runtime asset admission remains blocked.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This records the approved canonical Sharky identity and its reconstruction
references. It does not authorize runtime integration, production emotional
assets, a rig, motion, or a 3D mesh.

## 2. Authority hierarchy

`sharky_canonical_3q_front_authority_v1.png` is the sole visual authority.
SIDE, BACK, and 3/4 BACK are subordinate reconstruction references only. If
they conflict, the canonical 3/4 FRONT wins.

## 3. Approved canonical identity

Sharky is a compact premium pet-like shark companion: deep navy body, warm
cream underside, restrained teal accents, a warm observant blue/cyan eye
family, and a restrained asymmetric closed-mouth smile. The canonical Product
presentation language is 3/4 FRONT.

## 4. Anatomical construction

Use a short blunt shark-like muzzle, compact collected torso, short front fins,
balanced body-to-tail bridge, and one continuous side-swept tail. No legs,
feet, hips, humanoid anatomy, pelvic/ventral/lower support fins, slug-like
underside, seahorse waist, or thin dolphin peduncle.

## 5. Face and expression construction

Eyes are warm, observant blue/cyan; warm highlights are lighting reflections,
not heterochromia. Keep a modest asymmetric closed-mouth smile: no open-mouth
default, smugness, or random face/eye drift between states.

## 6. Crest and dorsal rules

One small soft asymmetric front crest (“messy tuft”), one main swept-back
dorsal fin, and one teal dorsal tip. Never add a second crest, horn-like crest,
or second dorsal fin.

## 7. Gill-mark placement and visibility

Exactly three slightly irregular teal gill marks exist on character-left only.
Never center them into a V, duplicate them, or mirror them.

## 8. Cream underside geometry

The warm cream underside is a compact front-plane identity cue. It must not
become a long flat underside or introduce humanoid lower anatomy.

## 9. Body-to-tail bridge and tail construction

The body transitions continuously to one balanced side-swept tail with a
single teal tail tip. No oversized teal region or inconsistent tail scale.

## 10. Palette direction

Deep navy is dominant; cream is warm and restrained; teal is an accent limited
to the dorsal tip, tail tip, and the three gill marks.

## 11. Material and lighting direction

Premium soft marine-skin material, subtle aquatic suspension, and restrained
soft lighting. Avoid a generic bright-blue preschool mascot, aggressive-real
shark, dolphin beak, seal profile, floor plane, or baked checkerboard.

## 12. Camera and presentation language

The approved 3/4 FRONT is canonical. Reconstruction views explain hidden
geometry but do not prove a single exact mesh.

## 13. Neutral-state contract

Calm, warm, available, and closed-mouth; no celebration or instruction gesture.

## 14. Future five-state production family

| State | Semantics |
| --- | --- |
| `neutralGuidance` | calm, warm, available |
| `teachingAttention` | observant toward clue/table; collaborative |
| `supportiveRepair` | safe after error; invites another look, never guilt/sadness |
| `recoveredAcknowledgement` | modest warmth after a proven repair |
| `earnedCompletion` | earned positive payoff, adult and premium |

All states must remain one identical character.

## 15. Mobile-size readability rules

Validate recognizability at 48px, 72px, 120px, and hero size; protect the
crest, eye family, cream plane, and three-gill-mark identity without clipping.

## 16. Forbidden drift

No generic preschool blue, dolphin/seal anatomy, aggressive realism, humanoid
parts, lower fins, mirrored gills, oversized teal, extra crest/dorsal, random
body/tail drift, or promotion of reconstruction references to authority.

## 17. Reconstruction-reference status

SIDE, BACK, and 3/4 BACK are accepted as owner-approved AI-assisted
reconstruction references, not runtime assets or exact multi-angle geometry.

## 18. Future professional 3D reconstruction contract

Build a professional sculpt, topology, rig, reproducible material system, and
animation-ready source model from this Bible with the 3/4 FRONT as authority.

## 19. Runtime export contract

Future exports require genuine PNG/WebP alpha, no checkerboard/floor/large
baked shadow, common canvas and registration, safe padding, deterministic
state names, manifest/provenance/checksums, dark-edge validation, and
repository-standard 1x/2x/3x density. UI owns shadows where practical.

## 20. Admission gates

The Canonical Identity Package is admitted. The Sharky Production System is
still blocked pending five matched, approved 3/4-front emotional production
assets and their state-to-runtime mapping review.
