## Principles worth studying

- poker table presented as one immersive scene rather than a widget above a separate lesson panel;
- restrained perspective and near/far depth;
- stronger spatial orientation of hero and opponents;
- pot, price, action and stack information positioned near the table object they describe;
- contextual coaching visually attached to the decision scene;
- clear depth hierarchy from environment to table to cards/HUD to coaching to controls;
- lower controls visually integrated with the table scene;
- premium emotional presentation without sacrificing decision clarity.

## Anti-answer-leak rule

Contextual coaching must direct attention without resolving the assessed decision for the learner.

Preferred:
`where/how to look -> learner interprets -> learner decides`.

Rejected:
`coach reveals the answer-bearing clue -> learner pattern-matches -> product counts it as independent skill`.
