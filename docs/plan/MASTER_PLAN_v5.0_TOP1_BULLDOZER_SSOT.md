# PART I — IMMUTABLE STRATEGY

## 1. Mission and outcome hierarchy

Sharky is a **premium, mobile-first, table-first poker learning system**: a coach, not a solver UI, gambling game, generic question bank, static trainer or chatbot. A learner must quickly know **what to observe, what to decide, why it matters, what was missed, how to repair it and how to prove the improvement**. Premium visual quality, brand attachment and engagement must strengthen the learning outcome rather than camouflage its absence.

**Operational North Star:** Turn a learner's poker mistake into a corrected decision that transfers to a materially different table situation and still holds after meaningful time.

`SEE → DECIDE → UNDERSTAND → REPAIR → PROVE → TRANSFER → RETAIN`

| Phase | Necessary learner behavior | Unacceptable substitute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEE | Independently notices the relevant table signal before the answer | The app highlights the answer-bearing clue first |
| DECIDE | Commits to a poker decision using available information | Guessing from option length, wording or animation |
| UNDERSTAND | Understands the causal reason and contextual limits | Generic “correct/wrong” or solver-frequency theater |
| REPAIR | Addresses the actual misconception and relevant missed signal | Random extra hands or generic reinforcement |
| PROVE | Independently chooses correctly with reduced scaffolding | Assisted correct counted as mastery |
| TRANSFER | Applies the skill in a materially new spot without leakage | The same question repeated with a different label |
| RETAIN | Reproduces the understanding after elapsed time | Same-session correctness called long-term learning |

**Automaticity** is an evaluated property of transfer — correct transfer with lower decision latency and **no accuracy loss** — not an extra engine or guaranteed short-term outcome.

**Priority of evidence:** real learner improvement > causal integrity > usability/trust > visual/brand distinction > engagement > feature breadth > internal code elegance. Technical safety is a **constraint**, not permission to endlessly optimize invisible infrastructure. The product succeeds when a learner makes better table decisions, understands why, returns for purposeful practice, and can eventually transfer the skill outside Sharky.

| `HUMAN_PROVEN` | A bounded observation of real novice understanding, usability or learning | Day-7 retention, population-wide impact or global #1 |
| `LONGITUDINALLY_PROVEN` | Retained human behavior across actual elapsed time | Real-poker generalization automatically |
| `REAL_POKER_PROVEN` | Better decisions on unseen, realistic, independently sourced poker spots | Real-money profitability or universal strategic mastery |

## 6. Minimum genuinely useful learning experience

The coherent learner path is:

`entry → short welcome/placement → one obvious next step → theory → independent decision → specific causal feedback → targeted repair → independent proof → session payoff → Review/return → transfer → retention`

**UI invariants:**

- The learner knows what is tappable, where to look, what information mattered and why the next rep was selected.
- No answer-bearing clue is pre-highlighted in assessment; no wording/option-quality leakage. Teaching help may be visible but is logged as help.
- Feedback differentiates **wrong**, **correct**, **suboptimal but playable**. Correctness is poker-state contextual, not oversimplified moral language.
- Repair targets the actual missed signal; Review shows unresolved vs resolved honestly, with no duplicate or ghost debt.
- A correct assisted answer gets encouraging teaching feedback without `Repair proved`/false transfer/retention.
- Independent recheck uses a valid owner, less scaffolding and an appropriate matched task; **same signal** is not necessarily a genuinely new **transfer**.
- One earned session closure communicates *what clue the learner now reads better*, what remains uncertain and one useful next action.
- Re-entry is sourced from actual prior error/proof, not random Daily reps, notification pressure or a fabricated weakness rating.
- Learner continuation takes precedence over interruptive global weakness redirect unless evidence establishes higher value.

**Each release-playable world** requires a clear objective, explainable terminology, concise theory, recognition practice, applied/transfer-ready task, honest correct/suboptimal/wrong feedback, at least one misconception/repair path, eligible independent recheck, earned world/lesson completion and coherent next-step handoff. Density means enough **differentiated cognitive practice** to learn, not a large screen/task count. Required sequential terminology and correct distractors are more valuable than bulk content authoring.
