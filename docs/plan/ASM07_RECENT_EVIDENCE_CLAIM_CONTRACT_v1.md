# ASM-07 recent evidence claim contract (implementation scope)

Owner disposition: preserve actual historical receipts/XP/achievements; separate latest source-verified responses from history. Missing support is **Confirmation needed**, not learner regression. No reconstruction of a past transient reinforcement certificate that was never stored. This contract does not change DoD acceptance, award a score, or authorize publication.

## Current evidence epochs
Use only the existing bounded recent evidence window (maximum200 stored records), never historical first-fact anchors or retention cache as current authority. Process unique actual records in original order; a missing interval begins a new available suffix. A known-family invalidator (incompatible answer/condition fingerprint, invalid identity/family/assistance/time) starts a new epoch for that family at its position. Unknown-family ambiguity starts new epochs for all families at its position. Later source-valid records can recover; older pre-invalidator successes cannot reappear. Preserve raw history and timestamps. No new storage, witness log, pruning or scheduling engine.

The current source oracle rederives grade, skill and family. Eligible confirmation attempts are timestamped, known-unassisted, non-placement and not legacy/unspaced, immediate repair or exact replay. The read clock is injectable; future/reversed time is not current support.

## One shared recent-verification predicate
Use the conjunction approved for this new semantic claim:
- >=5 eligible independent attempts in the family epoch;
- >=3 source-correct attempts; latest eligible attempt correct;
- >=2 tasks and >=2 sessions with existing minimum24h separation;
- the last two correct responses, in distinct sessions, >=24h apart within the existing latest4 eligible-response window.

These are existing Profile and transfer thresholds combined. The conjunction is deliberately stricter than old transfer and is NOT represented as unchanged semantics. Mixed factual counts may be shown; the claim is only **Recent verified responses**, never stable ability, improvement over a missing baseline, lifetime retention, mastery or Human proof. All-success evidence can recover without inventing a starting miss/debt.

“Recent” names the existing bounded response window, not a wall-clock freshness/retention promise. No new expiry threshold is invented; historical due scheduling and the strict complete-history retention projection retain their observed-clock expiry behavior.

Before the predicate is met, show factual counts when available plus an actionable **Confirmation needed** reason. Profile and FixProof use the same family confirmation state. A historical banked repair remains saved regardless of this current state. Do not turn a recent claim into a historical achievement or dynamically remove a real historical receipt. The existing `FirstEvidenceSignal` seed has no persisted event ledger: its transient “First evidence signal” label is excluded from the live recent-evidence earned section, while current verification remains visible in Profile/FixProof. No historical FirstEvidenceSignal certificate is fabricated or claimed preserved. Actual banked repair/achievement records remain untouched.

## Preserved historical owners
Raw evidence, XP/completion/earned-event history, debt, historical retention cache and due scheduling remain unchanged. The old complete-history current-retention projection is not used to claim retention from an unknown-prefix suffix. Existing arithmetic fixtures remain explicit test-only inputs, with a source guard prohibiting production bypass.

Functional English wording only; visual redesign/native proof deferred. Required evidence includes >200/repeated gaps and all-success recovery, family isolation, answer/condition invalidation, conflicting identities, time/assistance/replay boundaries, exact shared threshold boundaries, actual completion callbacks, Profile/FixProof consistency, old storage/seal/scheduling preservation, independent review and exact-candidate canonical checks.
