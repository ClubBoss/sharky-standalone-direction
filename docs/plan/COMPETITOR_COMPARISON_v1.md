# Sharky Poker competitor comparison

**Scope.** PLAN 0.3.2 (CMP-02 and CMP-03), checked on 23 November 2026. The audience is a new or early recreational Texas Hold'em player learning to make and explain a decision at the table. The five products come from K1. The Sharky baseline is public main at `0d64d29fe91fdaf612acc5e834559b6678aed5dd`. [PLAN](../PLAN.md) · [Baseline](https://github.com/ClubBoss/sharky-standalone-direction/tree/0d64d29fe91fdaf612acc5e834559b6678aed5dd)

Competitor capabilities come from their own public product pages and store listings. Sharky's evidence comes from its checked-in lessons, active route and tests. Each advantage below is a judgment about fit for the stated learning task. K1's CMP-01 installed-build comparison is a separate measurement. [PLAN, K1](../PLAN.md#маршрут-по-дням)

## Poker Skill

- **Significant loss.** A learner getting ready for a home game can practise consecutive decisions in a complete hand and review each decision afterwards. Poker Skill offers two- to five-player Sit & Gos against AI with live coaching and a hand-by-hand game review. Sharky's bot and coach work sits in a later PLAN stage. [Poker Skill features](https://www.pokerskill.com/features/) · [Sharky PLAN](../PLAN.md)
- **Confirmed Sharky advantage for an uninterrupted lesson session.** Poker Skill's lessons consume energy that refills over time. The Sharky W1–W12 course test covers completing every scheduled course and practice task, with retries, restarts and exact XP, through the ordinary lesson flow without an energy refill step. This supports the ability to continue through the core course in the same session. [Poker Skill lessons](https://www.pokerskill.com/features/#lessons) · [Sharky full-course test](../../test/ui_v2/act0_w1_long_session_v1_test.dart)

## River

- **Significant loss.** A learner ready to compare plausible actions can consult solver frequencies and expected value in preflop and postflop trainers, then ask Otto a follow-up in plain language. Sharky's current training gives authored reasons, such as the W4 call/fold explanations at fixed prices and stated chances. The conversational coach is later in PLAN. [River listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/river-poker-lessons-trainer/id6756941420) · [Sharky W4 examples](../../lib/ui_v2/act0_shell/act0_shell_runners_part3_v1.dart#L1154) · [Sharky PLAN](../PLAN.md)
- **Confirmed Sharky advantage for correcting a first rules error.** After a learner folds when there is no bet, the feedback can take them straight into a different drill on the same clue, then return to the source decision for a recheck. The live widget test follows that sequence from the feedback button through both correct answers. River's listing documents explanations and trainers. Sharky's linked sequence gives the novice a concrete next action inside the lesson. [River listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/river-poker-lessons-trainer/id6756941420) · [Sharky repair and recheck test](../../test/ui_v2/act0_repair_intent_lifecycle_v1_test.dart#L175)

## PokerPath

- **Significant loss.** A learner who understands the basics can practise at 6-max cash tables or in Sit & Gos against tight, loose, aggressive and solid bots. That gives a way to apply the same concept against different player styles in full hands. Sharky's core training here is authored decisions and scheduled practice; bots remain in a later stage. [PokerPath listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en&id=com.vicsothemes.pokerpath) · [Sharky course test](../../test/ui_v2/act0_w1_long_session_v1_test.dart) · [Sharky PLAN](../PLAN.md)
- **Confirmed Sharky advantage for checking whether a repair stuck.** PokerPath advertises recurring mistakes through Daily Review. Sharky makes the credit rule explicit: an immediate repair leaves retention pending, an independent due answer at Day 2 can schedule Day 7, and a replay or assisted answer cannot advance that schedule. A learner's correct retry and later independent success therefore carry different evidence. [PokerPath Daily Review](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en&id=com.vicsothemes.pokerpath) · [Sharky retention tests](../../test/ui_v2/act0_durable_retention_transfer_v1_test.dart#L88)

## Poker Power

- **Significant loss.** A new player who wants to practise speaking up, reading people and making choices under social pressure can join instructor-led classes with guided hands and discussion. The active Sharky route is a self-guided course on the phone. [Poker Power workshop FAQ](https://www.pokerpower.com/faqs) · [Sharky beta route](../RELEASE.md)
- **Confirmed Sharky advantage for self-paced technical instruction.** Poker Power describes its workshops as developing confidence, communication and judgment. A learner seeking technical Hold'em depth can follow Sharky's route from basic table reading to W12 decisions that compare call, raise, fold, bet and check using stated prices and opponent tendencies. The course test reaches both W12 checkpoint tasks after restarting in each world. [Poker Power workshop FAQ](https://www.pokerpower.com/faqs) · [Sharky W12 checkpoint scenarios](../../lib/ui_v2/act0_shell/act0_shell_runners_part4_v1.dart#L9876) · [Sharky course test](../../test/ui_v2/act0_w1_long_session_v1_test.dart)

## Runout

- **Significant loss.** A learner starting to study strategy across many spots can use Runout's advertised adaptive drills, decision accuracy by position and street, leak tagging and trends. Sharky's You surface reports course progress and repair evidence. Runout gives this learner more ways to locate a recurring mistake in their results. [Runout analytics](https://runoutpoker.com/#features) · [Sharky You runtime adapter](../../lib/ui_v2/act0_shell/act0_academy_runtime_adapter_v1.dart#L195)
- **Confirmed Sharky advantage for learning the price calculation from scratch.** Runout's published feedback example gives an EV score and discusses a 3-bet range. Sharky W4 exposes the numbers a beginner needs to recompute the choice. With 1 BB to call for an 11 BB pot and a stated 15% chance, calling clears the roughly 9.1% threshold. The companion spot asks for both action and threshold, then explains why a 25% chance falls short of about 31.6%. The pair isolates the same calculation in a profitable call and a fold. [Runout feedback example](https://runoutpoker.com/) · [Sharky W4 scenario pair](../../lib/ui_v2/act0_shell/act0_shell_runners_part3_v1.dart#L1154)
