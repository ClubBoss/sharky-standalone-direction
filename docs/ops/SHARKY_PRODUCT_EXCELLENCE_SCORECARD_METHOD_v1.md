## A. What “a banger Sharky” would mean

Sharky is excellent **only when its complete learner job** holds under actual use: a novice understands the situation, makes a real poker decision, learns the causal clue, corrects a misconception, independently applies it, retains it at a genuine later interval, and wants to perform another meaningful exercise. It must also feel premium, readable, coherent, emotionally rewarding and reliable **without confusing XP, synthetic demonstrations, screenshots, app opens or commercial interest with poker learning**.

Six non-substitutable success lenses:

| Lens | Question | Strongest legitimate proof | Cannot be proved by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **L — Learning truth** | Are choices, poker explanations, assistance, repair and completion actually correct? | Source contracts + tests + authenticated exact-binary native persisted receipts; independent review where required | A green test badge alone; XP; a pretty table |
| **U — Usability / clarity** | Can a real novice navigate and explain why a decision was right or wrong unaided? | Owner-admitted Human protocol, fixed app build, intervention/confusion ledger | Expert opinions and AI walkthroughs |
| **V — Visual / emotional trust** | Does the complete scene and surrounding Academy feel cohesive, legible and premium? | Accepted-binary standard + compact meaningful-state visual inspection; later real-person trust observation | Static mockup or historic V4 screenshot alone |
| **R — Genuine return and retention** | Do people return for a useful exercise and remember/apply the clue after actual elapsed time? | Source-owned due + real elapsed user practice, matched recheck and materially new independent spot; cohorts if claimed | D7 app opens alone; synthetic clock claiming natural retention |
| **C — Competitive edge** | Does Sharky equal or beat relevant alternatives on the *same beginner job*? | Named competitor/version/date, matched task, reproducible human evaluation and limitations | Feature lists, marketing claims, unqualified 10/10 |
| **S — Ship / premium readiness** | Is the intended offer safe, honest, technically reliable and worth buying? | Real-device/RC evidence, age/privacy/access disclosures, commerce proof if applicable, actual market tests | Screenshots, stated pricing intention or friend feedback |

**No single global “ready %” is an authority.** For steering decisions, display the six lenses, the active accepted gate, unresolved P0/P1 and the one next evidence-backed bottleneck. The product is not “a banger” merely because every screen has a high design score.

## B. Scoring without false precision

**Use a 0–4 ordinal quality rubric per named measurable job, NOT a 0–100 launch percentage.** `UNVERIFIED` is distinct from 0. Use the same rubric for later comparable reviews; no automatically computed average is accepted-stage proof.

| Level | Meaning | Minimum supporting evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `UNVERIFIED` | No admissible evidence for the stated job/claim; **not** a failure score | Record missing evidence and next test; no numeric grade |
| **0 — Material failure** | Exact applicable evidence confirms the job is materially incorrect, misleading or blocked | Repro steps, exact source/binary, impact and classification |
| **1 — Fragile** | Works only intermittently/with expert guidance or has major user cost | Exact repro or bounded authentic observation |
| **2 — Functional, limited** | Core job works on a proven scoped route, but some relevant states/clarity/cohesion remain open | Named route/state, matching receipts, explicit coverage limits |
| **3 — Strong, integrated** | Consistently clear and robust on the relevant complete journey, meaningful states and form factors, with appropriate Human evidence where this claim requires it | Integrated real-binary + admissible role-specific evidence; unresolved material issues absent in that scope |
| **4 — Differentiated / exceptional** | Level 3 **plus** demonstrable, meaningful advantage over a named alternative on a matched learner job, without quality/reliability tradeoff | Fair matched benchmark and appropriately qualified user outcome; comparative method + date |

**Proof level is a separate column.** Record `SOURCE`, `MACHINE_NATIVE`, `HUMAN`, `LONGITUDINAL`, `REAL_POKER`, `MARKET`, or their combination. Do not give a higher Q to an unobserved app or use `MACHINE_NATIVE` to justify `HUMAN`. If a criterion is fundamentally Human/longitudinal, `SOURCE` alone can establish only narrower technical coverage, not a final 3 or 4 for that claim. A “4” on technical stability does not imply a “4” on real-poker skill.

Optional management shorthand: `Q2/MACHINE_NATIVE/PARTIAL` or `Q=UNVERIFIED/HUMAN_PENDING`. A missing Human result does **not** automatically downgrade an otherwise evidenced mechanical subclaim; split the claims instead.

## E. Competitive benchmark method — compare jobs, not feature counts

At the existing **S18 Competitive Excellence Gate**, use a minimal fair benchmark panel chosen for the job, not all apps every time. The 2026-10-14 source list is in `03_SHARKY_MARKET_COMPETITIVE_RESEARCH_20261014.md`; **vendor-documented capabilities are hypotheses about their product, not observed 2026 binary parity**.

Suggested comparator candidates, pending verified current accessible build/version:

- **Runout / Poker Skill:** novice-first poker orientation, first choice, pedagogy and premium first session.
- **PeakGTO / GTO Wizard:** real-error diagnosis, contextual poker reasoning, practice/recheck and depth at the appropriate skill level; advanced workflows may be out of scope for a novice benchmark.
- **Chessable / Chess.com:** retrieval practice, useful next rep, replay/due clarity; only analogues, **not poker-correctness competitors**.

**Matched protocol:** record app/version/date/device/language/payment access; define a single task and intended learner level; have participants use real products under equivalent instructions; capture intervention, task completion, explanation accuracy, time-to-first-meaningful-value, error recovery, perceived clarity and confidence **without pretending small-N implies population leadership**. Compare category-relevant jobs; never rate Sharky #1 from brand screenshots or vendor feature counts. `PARITY / BETTER_ON_THIS_JOB / WORSE_ON_THIS_JOB / UNTESTED` is preferable to invented decimal “10/10”. Revisit only if S18/Human evidence supports doing so.

## F. Delta and governance protocol

**Three different ledgers must not be averaged into a release percentage:**

1. **`A` — Accepted Readiness (0–100 within an *already defined admitted stage*, if a stage outcome budget exists):** increases only for its accepted, evidence-backed DoD; a still-open blocking gate cannot be recorded as PASSED. Preserve the stage's predeclared scope, baseline and acceptance rule. **No cross-stage sum**.
2. **`W` — Earned Execution Work (0–100 within that admitted stage):** pre-budgeted verified outputs such as reproduced causal defect, bounded code fix, tests and native receipts. May advance under HOLD. A diagnosis does not substitute for a repair. No credit from document merge or test rerun alone.
3. **`Q` — Surface/outcome quality ordinal (0–4, or `UNVERIFIED`):** reviewed independently per named job, with evidence class and scope. Grade changes only for comparable evidence and a reproducible change in user job quality; not because a PR was opened or a scorecard was reformatted.

Additional **coverage indicator**: `# required claims with admissible evidence / # claims in admitted scope`, labelled with the scope. **Coverage is not quality**. If the method, category set, weights, cohort or task changes, mark `REBASE` and show old+new frames; do not present the methodological difference as improvement.

**Standard row — store only evidenced state when an authorized workstream creates it:**

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `date`, `owner`, `scope`, `stage` | Observer and exact authorized boundary |
| `HEAD/TREE`, `binary`, `artifact`, `native device/profile` | Reproducible candidate identity (if applicable) |
| `job_id`, `scenario/task IDs`, `evidence class` | What was actually tested, not an aggregate impression |
| `status_before → status_after` | `UNVERIFIED / PARTIAL / PASS_SCOPED / HOLD_MATERIAL / DEFERRED / NOT_APPLICABLE` as appropriate |
| `Q_before → Q_after`, `ΔQ` | Quality delta, or `N/A` if incomparable; no speculative numbers |
| `A_before → A_after`, `ΔA` | Stage accepted outcome, within its authorized budget; `+0` when only work earned |
| `W_before → W_after`, `ΔW` | Budgeted work outcome, with no double credit |
| `coverage_before → coverage_after` | Proven applicable cases, not a blanket coverage claim |
| `evidence_link + falsifier + limitation` | Actual receipts, unresolved behavior, confidence and stop condition |
| `next single bottleneck`, `owner admission required?` | Routing suggestion, not a second roadmap |

**Example of correct notation (illustrative, NOT Sharky's current numbers):**

`Job=Session Summary | status: PARTIAL → PARTIAL | Q: UNVERIFIED → Q2 [SOURCE+NATIVE, clean case only] | A: 30→30 (+0) | W: 40→46 (+6 pre-budgeted) | coverage: 1/3→2/3 | missing: mixed-assistance saved state | next: independent persisted proof.`

If a new source or UX proof reveals that an older quality rating was incorrect, report `REBASE / REVOKED_CLAIM` with evidence—not fabricated negative progress. For regression, grade falls only when the same relevant observed job actually deteriorates. Historical version scores (for example a dated 18-surface 53/100 subjective index) remain **historical observations, not the starting point of this rubric**.

## H. Falsification and stop rules

- **DO NOTHING is a real option.** If no meaningful learner-facing defect exists, avoid fresh design/refactor merely to raise a metric.
- A material wrong poker/assistance/false progress state overrides a high visual Q.
- A missing required Human proof stays `UNVERIFIED` even if AI reviewers agree enthusiastically.
- Side-by-side visual parity or one pleasant session never establishes lasting learning.
- No daily XP/streak/rank engine, generic AI personalization, huge content program or independent renderer can be admitted by this scorecard.
- Competing drafts, historical project attachments and archived scores do not supersede the live Master Plan. Do not restart historic V4 screenshot micro-iterations or merge PR #224 without a separate owner gate.
- Freeze method version for comparable observations. Amend only when an actual measurement blind spot is demonstrated; log a versioned `METHOD_CHANGE / REBASE` and retain earlier observations by provenance.
