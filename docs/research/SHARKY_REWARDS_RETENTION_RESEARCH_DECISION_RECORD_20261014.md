## 1. Owner-facing synthesis

Preserve the existing learning engine and reward rules. Explore alternative presentation only where current native and later Human evidence identifies a learner comprehension or motivation failure.

**DO NOTHING is a valid winner.** Existing Sharky behavior already contains XP, earned evidence seeds, streak state, a source-owned personalized return reason, assistance-aware graded records, error→repair→independent recheck and durable review. Research does not prove this implementation optimal, nor justify replacing it.

## 2. Evidence quality and limitations

- **Market:** 14 relevant public-product comparisons as of 2026-10-14 (poker, chess, education, coaching). Current vendor help/features prove *documented mechanics*, **not** independent app-binary behavior or a causal retention/learning lift. Public D1/D7, subscription conversion and learning-effect attribution generally undisclosed.
- **Behavioral science:** 19 selected studies/reviews offer comparatively stronger evidence for *feedback specificity, practice retrieval and spaced review* than for any precise XP, daily streak or achievement design. Samples are usually not adult poker novices; heterogeneous pooled effect sizes are **not forecasts** for Sharky.
- **Existing repository:** The Sol report inspected main on 2026-10-14, including achievement seeds, personalized return projection and Summary consumers. Claude read newer authority docs but some reward-code observations came from an older August checkout. Prefer current main, then final accepted source/native, over the latter for implementation claims.
- **Source-specific UI hypothesis:** In the examined current main, \`_BlockXpProgressCardV1\` uses a 0/100% bar based on whether \`xpEarned > 0\`. It is **not a mastery computation**. Whether learners mistake the full bar for mastery is untested. Check the real Summary at the appropriate visual/Human gate; no S11 interruption or automatic UI ticket.
- **Strongest research uncertainty:** an evidence-forward Summary might improve accurate understanding without increasing voluntary return, and immediate XP may be more rewarding in the first session before any delayed skill proof exists. Preserve emotional recognition without counterfeit achievement claims.

### Claims that remain unproven

- XP value/rate optimality or its causal benefit for meaningful return.
- Daily streak feature attribution, positive or negative, in actual Sharky learners.
- H2 evidence-first UX superiority over the *current* implemented baseline.
- Genuine independent recall after elapsed 24h / 72h / 7d, unseen transfer and real-poker performance.
- D7/D30 retention, subscriptions, willingness to pay or commercial causal uplift.

Do not conflate opens, session completion, qualified reps, independently retained skill and payment. The metric families have different proof owners.

## 3. Future hypotheses (no new gate or tests now)

**H-R1 — Comprehension / payoff:** On one authentic accepted learner record, compare *existing* Summary/Profile with a minimal evidence-forward explanation (no-code prototype only if existing comprehension fails). Ask a participant without prompting what was learned, what involved help, what was independently established and what is still unresolved. Check emotional reward and next-action clarity. The unchanged current UI is the control.

**H-R2 — Return job:** On true real-time return, observe whether an existing genuinely unresolved/due signal produces a voluntary relevant independent rep, as distinct from generic \`Continue\`. Check source/task identity and subsequent correctness. Never simulate elapsed retention as natural.

**H-R3 — Retained/transfer proof:** After real elapsed intervals, separately assess the same-signal recheck and a materially new poker spot. Do not assert "stable mastery" from one successful repetition.

**H-R4 — Reward pressure:** Observe whether current streak/XP/achievement chrome promotes guessing, anxiety, or collecting activity rather than handling harder decisions. Remove/reframe only on reproduced harm.

**H-R5 — Commercial value:** At the separately admitted market stage, test whether additional legitimate depth and repeated training—not friction currencies—offers a meaningful reason to pay; actual purchase evidence is separate.

Use existing telemetry/source owners where sufficient. Research does not authorize new schemas or cohorts today. The future owner selects valid methods, sample size and ethical recruitment for the relevant gate.
