Academic references are different units of evidence, not 19 additional competitive products. Two independent agents agreeing on a direction does not create an independent randomized Sharky outcome.

### Quality rules for future updates

1. **Master Plan > live execution context > rules/protocol > subordinate research.** A draft PR is not adopted on main until valid merge.
2. Prefer current exact source and independently attested native artifact to an older local checkout or a screenshot caption.
3. Prefer independent observed behavior over vendor efficacy marketing; distinguish current documentation from installed binary and historical release.
4. Identify study population, measured outcome, control, follow-up, uncertainty and transfer limits before quoting uplift.
5. Separate activity/opens, meaningful unaided reps, delayed recall, novel-spot transfer, real-poker decisions and willingness to pay.
6. If a source lacks a datum, label it \`UNKNOWN / NOT DISCLOSED\`. Do not manufacture retention, mastery, business or engineering ROI percentages.
7. No duplicated reward/state owner, new daily engine or XP redesign without admitted evidence of product EV.
8. When Human or market evidence exists, append a **new dated record** with a source/binary identity and falsification outcome instead of retroactively editing these reports to claim they predicted it.
