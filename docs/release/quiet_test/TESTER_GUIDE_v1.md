# Sharky — quiet test

**Thank you for helping us find what is unclear in this early version.**
We are testing the app, not your poker knowledge. Mistakes are useful.

**Install.** Use the iPhone with Sharky already installed by the owner, or
follow their invitation to install the agreed test build. Wait at the Home
Screen until the owner says you can begin. Allow about 20–30 minutes.
The owner will ask for your permission before recording the app screen;
you may decline recording or stop the session at any time.

**Your task.**

- Open Sharky and start learning as you normally would. Use the English app.
- Work through World 1 (W1), using the explanations, hints and retries offered
  by the app. Make choices yourself; there is no need to get them all right.
- If you get stuck, try whatever you would normally try. You may describe
  what you are looking for or what you think a message means.
- Stop when you finish W1, cannot continue, or want to stop. Leave the last
  screen open if possible and tell the owner you are finished.

**During the test.** Please do not ask the owner which answer is right, how to
play a hand, or which button to choose. Do not search for answers elsewhere.
The owner will not teach or steer you during the session. You can always ask
for technical help with the phone, privacy, or stopping the test.

At the end, the owner will ask five short feedback questions. Your honest
experience is more useful than a positive review.
