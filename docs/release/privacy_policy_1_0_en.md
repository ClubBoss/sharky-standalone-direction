# Sharky Poker Privacy Policy

For privacy questions, use the developer contact on the Sharky Poker App Store support page.

## Data on your iPhone

Sharky Poker runs without an account. The app stores your lesson and practice
progress on your device, including answers, help used, mistakes and repeat
attempts, XP, study dates, your place in the course and your settings. It keeps
local recovery copies so it can restore your progress after an interrupted
save. The public app does not send these records to Sharky or to analytics,
advertising or AI providers. It does not use cross-app tracking.

If you enable the practice reminder and grant notification permission, iOS
schedules a local reminder. You can turn the reminder off in Sharky or revoke
notification permission in iOS Settings.

## Retention and deletion

Sharky keeps your local progress and settings between sessions. The recent
answer history has a limit. As you practice, new entries can replace older
entries.

To erase your progress and settings on this device, open **You → Settings →
Delete local data**. This also clears local recovery copies and cancels the
practice reminder. If the device cannot finish clearing the data, the app asks
you to retry. Local deletion cannot be undone.

Your iPhone or iCloud backup settings may include app data. Apple manages those
backups. Sharky’s local deletion does not remove older device backups or copies
you saved or shared outside the app.

## Support and Apple services

If you contact us, we use the email address and information you choose to send
to answer your request, investigate the issue and follow up. Please include as
little personal information as possible. The support mailbox provider processes
messages to deliver and store them for us. We keep these messages for as long
as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations. Use the
support contact to request access, correction or deletion of the information you
sent to support.

Apple manages download and update data, as well as diagnostics you choose to
share through Apple’s settings, under its own privacy policy. Apple may make
crash reports or aggregate App Store statistics available to the developer. We
use those reports to diagnose reliability issues. You can manage analytics
sharing in iOS Settings. [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Updates

We publish changes to this policy here with the effective date. You can also
find Sharky’s privacy information in **You → Settings → Privacy Policy**.
