# SHARKY — Lean Growth & Subscription Product Decision
**Owner-direction record, 2026-10-12 · Strategy input, not a new roadmap or product gate**

## 1. Business outcome and deliberate non-goals

Build a premium, easy-to-use mobile poker-learning product people voluntarily revisit, recommend, and ultimately find worth paying for. The user-facing experience should feel intelligent; the runtime should remain intentionally simple and deterministic. This is **not** an authorization to build a general-purpose AI, solver platform, new content engine, social network, badges economy or speculative multi-ledger personalization system.

**Objective:** verified improvements in poker decisions and enjoyment **plus** a credible recurring learner job, genuine organic discovery and demonstrated willingness to pay, at the lowest maintainable engineering/coordination cost. Subscription, virality, Day-1/Day-7 retention and human transfer are **hypotheses, not achieved results**. Technical correctness and fraud-resistant progress are non-negotiable constraints, not a business outcome in themselves.

Priority in execution: learner first value → actual ability improvement/transfer → useful return → lightweight organic discovery → real recurring paid value. Prefer a measurable reduction in time-to-useful-learning over a large number of new abstractions, PRs or tests.

## 2. Selected lean product model

**KEEP the owner-approved A+ Guided Academy with the existing Review/Practice as one deterministic personalized Decision Lab.** This is one learner product, not two parallel systems. Academy = a clear learning path for novices; Decision Lab = one relevant next rep based on already owned mistake/due/progress evidence; Sharky Coach = short, accurate, encouraging explanation drawn from current contracts (not a separate conversational AI system).

- **First session:** target 2–5 minutes to a real, independently attempted and understood poker clue, with a truthful follow-up attempt. This is a UX hypothesis for later Human verification, not evidence of current first-value timing.
- **Return:** one meaningful current next action, especially a valid previously missed clue/independent repair or a genuinely due review. If none is due, offer an honest available lesson or optional practice; no fake urgency, streak penalties or invented debt.
- **Proven progress:** concise saved result and 2–3 source-backed facts in You/Profile; distinguish source retry correctness from unfinished mapped transfer and assisted learning from independent proof.
- **Long-term use:** subject to content coverage and user validation, recurring novel-context practice may provide value after finite Academy content. Reuse authored, poker-valid situations across teaching, remediation and spaced review **only when pedagogically independent**, not by relabeling repeated answers.
- **Scope:** W1–W4 free Foundation, W5–W12 intended paid deeper curriculum under current monetization SSOT; no price, purchase, trial or entitlement is approved by this memo. W1–W12 remains the intended complete Volume I launch-quality bar unless owner explicitly admits a narrower honest beta.

## 3. Growth and revenue assumptions to falsify cheaply

The 2026-10-12 cross-industry research compared 13 examples (poker trainers; Chess.com/Chessable/Aimchess; Brilliant/Duolingo/Mimo/Elevate; Finch/Strava/Headspace). Strategic synthesis favored Academy + recurring practice, but **does not establish Sharky's conversion, TAM, viral coefficient, churn, D7 or willingness to pay**. Rival free daily challenges make “daily question + explanation” an insufficient **assumed** premium differentiator; only tested users can adjudicate the offer.

**Research hypotheses and lowest-cost probes, admitted only at existing Human/Market gates:**

| Hypothesis | Cheapest valid probe | Measure; fail signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First value is immediate | 5-minute first-use novice observation on eligible build/prototype | Independent choice, articulated causal clue, successful second context; confusion or assisted-only success falsifies |
| User wants to return | Voluntary 24h / Day-7 recheck after real elapsed time | Actual reopened meaningful reps/retained understanding, not notification taps or fake clock |
| Users organically share | Manually authored *One Spot, Two Decisions* challenge with no app/social integration | Real voluntary sends → recipients making a choice/discussing reasoning, not merely share-button clicks |
| Free/paid boundary is legible | Two honest neutral offer mockups after experienced first value | Can users correctly distinguish Free/Plus and name the incremental benefit? |
| Ongoing practice merits subscription | Interview/price choice after multiple authentic sessions, eventual actual purchase experiment only if separately admitted | Specific repeated learner need, actionable price-choice evidence; if weak, evaluate one-time courses/packs instead of forcing subscriptions |

Initial small-sample decision thresholds in the research are **heuristics**, not estimates of mass conversion, retention, viral growth or LTV. No false checkout, unauthorized email acquisition, real-person claims from synthetic testers or new analytics vendor. Minimum source-owned opt-in events suffice for early routes.

## 4. Anti-rocket implementation and verification rules

1. **No new general engine without a real learner failure:** before proposing a new service/state/projection/schema, show the failing existing-user journey, identify the current authoritative owner, propose the simplest source-consumer fix and quantify expected product EV. If existing rules/copy/valid content can achieve the outcome, reuse them.
2. **No future-tech tax:** defer universal AI Coach, ML, solver/hand import, W13–W36 scale-up, automated content factory, daily engine, streak recovery economy, public leaderboard, friends graph and referral backend until evidence and owner admission justify each.
3. **Big outcome-sized Gauntlets:** one active writable production owner; implement an entire admitted learner job, not an arbitrary small file quota. Up to ~2 causally bounded rework loops per defect class, then one manager/root-cause decision rather than endless repairs. Reserve time/quota for integration, exact-head CI and one independent acceptance at the real gate.
4. **Proof proportional to risk:** reuse unchanged accepted evidence, run focused tests and early native smoke when learning path is affected; final exact-binary checks where the Master Plan requires them. Do not fabricate PASS, silently merge, retest frozen visuals or use tests-for-tests. Historical inherited failures remain honestly disclosed.
5. **Every big wave answers five questions:** Which learner/return/organic-growth/payment problem is targeted? Why is current source/copy/content insufficient? What's the cheapest falsifier? What user-visible action proves the improvement? What is explicitly deferred?
6. **Stop on diminishing value:** an unblocked next approved product phase takes precedence over speculative framework perfection. A material P0/P1 learner-integrity defect does not become optional for speed.
