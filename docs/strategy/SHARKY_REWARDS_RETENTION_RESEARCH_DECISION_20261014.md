## 1. Evidence hierarchy and what is **not** proven

- **Verified source-level facts:** Act0 has XP and once-only awards, deterministic achievement seeds/consumers, streak-related data, progress/Review, feedback and source-owned personalized next-action logic. These are capabilities, **not** tested learner outcomes.
- **Competitor research:** official poker/chess/education/habit product pages substantiate *claimed or documented mechanisms* (e.g., leak-linked drills, spaced retrieval, streaks, XP); they do not establish live build behavior, causal app retention, skill transfer or subscription conversion. Vendor A/B outcomes, including the historic Duolingo streak-rule comparison, are specific to their interventions/populations.
- **Behavioral science:** informative task-level feedback, corrective learning, independent retrieval and spacing have stronger general pedagogical evidence than a specific XP badge/streak formula; heterogeneous research involving students, gyms and consumer behavior cannot validate effects for adult poker novices.
- **Unknown:** optimal authored XP amounts or scheduling intervals, gains in Sharky D1/D7/D30, independent retained poker ability, real-table transfer, premium conversion and willingness to pay. There is **no** measured causal Sharky uplift.

## 3. Source-grounded consumer-level falsifiers

1. Current-main `_BlockXpProgressCardV1` maps `summary.xpEarned <= 0 ? 0.0 : 1.0` to a 0%/100% progress indicator. This is source-visible, **not** a verified user misconception. Test whether it appears to mean full mastery on an accepted native Summary.
2. Current Act0 has streak/achievement presentation paths. Whether the actual user feels guilt/pressure or useful continuity is **unverified**; do not infer it merely from a state field or an archived UI screenshot.
3. Early sessions have little delayed evidence; an immediate short, earned acknowledgment can be useful while higher-quality delayed proof has not matured.
4. Keep `One Spot, Two Decisions`, repair and Review jobs differentiated rather than adding superficial daily tasks.
