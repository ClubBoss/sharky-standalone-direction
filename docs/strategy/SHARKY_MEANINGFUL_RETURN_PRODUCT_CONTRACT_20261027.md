## 1. Product decision and user promise

The owner wants Sharky to be **highly enjoyable to revisit**, not merely an accurate poker-learning utility. The solution must improve both (a) enjoyment/recognition of effort and (b) genuinely useful return to learning. The two do not cancel each other.

**North Star:** `Learn → Feel progress → Return for a meaningful next step → Prove it`.

We will treat motivation as a **whole-journey product quality**, not as a gallery of badges inside You alone. The first sessions must deliver prompt, truthful positive feedback even before delayed retention evidence exists. Avoid compulsive loss-aversion pressure or variable/random reward mechanics reminiscent of gambling.

**Decision today:** KEEP once-only XP, current levels, supported earned moments, source-selected next action, authentic world unlocks, causal feedback, genuine repair/due eligibility, persistence and profile learning proof. **Conditionally REFINE presentation** where the native journey or user comprehension shows material opportunity. **DEFER** new streak economies, seven-login medals, XP boosts, daily-task debt, reward currencies, rank leagues, infinite badge catalogs, new notifications and social competition. `DO_NOTHING` remains eligible if a proposed variant is not better.

The 2026-10-14 research decision remains valid. Market figures are **competitor evidence**, not Sharky uplift forecasts. No claim of high Sharky D7/D30, real-poker improvement or purchase intent exists.

## 2. Four moments of value, across the actual app

| Moment | User experience | Existing truthful source | Location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Immediate | A compact, warm recognition of a real correct choice; assisted help is recognized only as teaching | Grading, answer/correctness, assistance, once-only XP | Decision feedback, first session |
| Session | A clear statement of what was attempted, independently correct, assisted, missed or repaired; earned reward secondary | Run summary, evidence ledger, source completion and XP | Summary, return to Home |
| Accumulated | A premium, modestly celebratory personal history of completed tasks, banked fixes, later reinforcement, real earned moments | `Act0ProfileStateV1`, `Act0AchievementSeedConsumerV1`, `Act0CrossSessionProfileProofV1`, `Act0ProfileEvidenceProjectionV1` | You, Learn/Worlds |
| Delayed | Invitation to an **actually available** independent recheck or honest next lesson/rep; no manufactured due debt | Source-owned task ID and due scheduler, repair ledger, next useful action | Home, Review, optional Practice |

**Experience contract:**
- **Welcome / Placement:** a credible, low-friction promise and a real start; ungraded demo/intake creates no phantom XP or mastery.
- **Home:** exactly one justified next action, clear about *why* it is useful; no competing daily chore board.
- **Learn / Worlds:** make a source-earned unlock satisfying, without moving or inventing unlock eligibility.
- **Practice:** short voluntary source-routed reps; no rival Review/repair economy or fake `complete today's missions`.
- **Feedback / Summary:** the highest-value place to improve emotional payoff. Keep prompt truthful positive feedback, reveal a specific clue or acquired ability, keep XP secondary; inspect the currently source-observed XP-card full-bar behavior on the exact native state before modifying it.
- **Review:** separate saved-error correction from genuinely eligible independent due checking; never spoil the correct answer at entry, reward opening a screen, or equate guided teaching with mastery.
- **You:** a restrained premium earned-record presentation (personal identity + source-backed tasks, banked repairs, later supporting evidence, and a few legitimately earned moments), with helpful new-learner empty state. Not an invented rating/leaderboard or badge shop.
- **Settings:** only real controls, including any actually supported accessibility/sound/preferences; no placeholder notification toggles.

## 3. Evidence and language contract (applies to all screens)

1. One source owns the event: stable world/lesson/task/run/session IDs, actual `user_choice`, correctness/error type, timing when valid, assistance provenance, persisted award/repair/due eligibility. No duplicate reward ledger.
2. **Distinct evidence tiers:** attempted/completed; assisted teaching; independent same/known spot; unseen transfer; real elapsed retention; cross-session reinforcement. A visual label cannot promote a weaker class into mastery.
3. First use may celebrate a **genuine first independently correct read** without claiming lesson completion, skill mastery or retention. Errors may yield positive *teaching* feedback, not an unearned proficiency medal.
4. XP is activity recognition and awarded once from the existing engine. Badges/moments appear only when existing source thresholds are met; don't add awards for visiting the app, clicking tabs, screenshots or artificial clock advancement.
5. User can leave after one worthwhile session. Never manufacture urgency or use guilt/threats around streak loss. Prefer one meaningful voluntary next action.
6. New or empty users get encouraging, honest content without zero-score shame; sparse evidence stays sparse; no guessed skill percentage. Preserve no-spoiler independent checks.
7. Native preview/mock fixtures are labeled illustrative. Green CI, injected screenshots, view counts and chosen visual direction are not learner outcomes.

## 5. Evaluation approach (definitions, not measured Sharky metrics)

**Candidate primary engagement outcome — Meaningful Return:** proportion of an *explicitly defined cohort* returning on a subsequent real civil day/window and completing at least one valid source-owned learning decision (right **or wrong**) or authorized independent due check, with a persisted eligible record. Do **not** classify an app open, tab visit, claimable badge, XP-only event, screenshot fixture, automatic due scheduling, or merely viewing an answer as meaningful learning return. Cohort denominator, day-window and uniqueness rule must be pre-specified before analysis; use existing privacy-safe events where possible.

**Keep separate:** D1/D7/D30 app return; qualified voluntary learning return; naturally eligible due-check execution; independent accuracy; new-context transfer; actual elapsed recall/retention; perceived competence/enjoyment; consumer value/WTP. Never mix these into a single unexplained "retention score".

**Cheapest checks before implementation:**
1. On actual EN14Pro: ask a novice to explain the current Summary and You without prompts, distinguish XP/activity from proof, identify the next real action, and report whether the result feels rewarding or pressuring.
2. If a credible comprehension/motivation gap appears, compare identical authentic source records across current vs restrained evidence-forward mockup; keep unchanged candidate as valid.
3. After permitted Human admission, predefine useful-return and learning outcomes, observe *real* later behavior; judge psychological pressure, comprehension, and earning integrity alongside engagement.

No new telemetry SDK or broad tracking migration is admitted by this document. If existing records lack a necessary privacy-safe field, separately scope the smallest gap.
